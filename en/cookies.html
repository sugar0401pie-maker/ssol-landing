What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. “First-party” cookies are set by us; “third-party” cookies are set by other companies. Websites can also store small settings in your browser's local storage.

In our services

Our services use a few essential cookies to run, such as keeping you signed in, remembering results saved without an account, completing payments and preventing abuse (set by SSOL, Toss Payments and PayPal). SSOL Astro House can also use analytics (Google Analytics) and ad-measurement (X) cookies, but only if you say yes with the optional checkbox, and you can change your mind any time under Cookie settings in that service's footer. We never send your birth details, nickname, answers, email address or phone number to Google or X. Each service's cookie notice has the details.

Your choices

Optional cookies stay off until you allow them. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, sign-in and payment will not work. To limit personalized ads more widely, see your X ad settings, www.youronlinechoices.eu (EU) or optout.aboutads.info (US). California residents: turning off advertising cookies is how you opt out of “sharing” under California law. We also honor Global Privacy Control signals.
