This policy covers the English pages of our website at www.ssolwellnesshouse.com/en. It follows Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and, for visitors in those regions, the EU and UK GDPR and California privacy law.

SSOL Wellness House offers non-clinical wellness coaching and wellness sessions. We don't provide psychotherapy, diagnosis or medical treatment.

Our services have their own policies

SSOL Astro House, SSOL Tarot House, Sowella AI and our 1:1 wellness sessions collect more information, such as answers, readings, chat messages and payment details. Each service explains what it collects, who receives it and how long it keeps it in its own privacy policy, published inside the service. Where this page and a service's policy differ about that service, the service's policy applies.

At a glance

  • No account needed to read this website.
  • We never sell your data. We share it only with the services that run the site for us.
  • Your data may leave Korea. Our website is hosted in the United States, and some of our services use providers in the United States and India.
  • You stay in control. You can see, fix or delete your data, and withdraw consent, at any time.

How long we keep it

We delete your data as soon as we no longer need it. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer you and as the law requires. Website access records are kept by our hosting provider under its standard retention period. Some records must be kept longer by Korean law; each service's policy lists the periods that apply to it. When a period ends, we erase electronic files so they cannot be recovered.

How we protect it

  • Management: an internal privacy plan, as few staff with access as possible, regular training.
  • Technical: access rights control, access control systems, encryption of personal information, security software kept up to date.
  • Physical: restricted access to places where personal information is stored.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Our English website and services are for adults aged 18 and over and may not be suitable for minors. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that we have, we stop their use and delete the data. If you think a minor has given us data, write to contact@ssolwellness.com.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • see the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, or ask us to stop or limit using it;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what we did before.

Email contact@ssolwellness.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We act without delay and reply within one month at the latest.

EU and UK residents also have the right to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents also have the right to know what categories of data we collect, to opt out of “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We honor Global Privacy Control signals.

Korean residents can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118, privacy.kisa.or.kr), the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972, www.kopico.go.kr), the Supreme Prosecutors' Office (1301, www.spo.go.kr) or the Korean National Police Agency (182, ecrm.cyber.go.kr).

Changes to this policy

We post changes here at least 7 days before they take effect. Changes that seriously affect your rights, such as new data items or new recipients, are posted at least 30 days ahead, and we ask for new consent where the law requires.
