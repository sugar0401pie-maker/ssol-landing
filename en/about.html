How a chat flows

Sowella AI isn't a chatbot that just waits for you. Like a coach, it forms a hunch, checks it with you, and only then suggests something.

  1. 1Starts with what it knows — It begins with your test results and what you shared when you signed up.
  2. 2Safety first — Every message is checked against safety rules first.
  3. 3Opens with your own answers — Your first chat builds on the answers you chose.
  4. 4Narrows things down — It narrows the possible reasons behind your worry, one or two at a time.
  5. 5Checks with you — It asks, “It sounds like… does that fit?” and only works with what you confirm.
  6. 6Picks an approach and a step — It chooses the approach that fits the reason, and a small step that fits you now.
  7. 7Sets the pace — It matches the step to how much you have in you right now.
  8. 8Follows up — It checks whether you tried the step, and adjusts with you.
  9. 9Connects you to a person — If you want to go deeper, it can connect you to a wellness session with a trainer.

Sowella AI You mentioned you've been feeling low for no clear reason. Since you like running, how about trying something like a running club?

The same “I don't like group workouts” can come from different reasons, and each reason gets a different suggestion. If you're worried people will see you struggle, it suggests setting a “good enough” bar instead of a perfect one. Safety

Every conversation passes through a “safety router” first. It's designed not to mistake everyday phrases for a crisis, while never missing real warning signs.

See how it responds, case by case
If you say…Sowella AI…
An everyday phrase like “I just want to quit everything”Asks what happened before showing crisis resources, and keeps track of whether it repeats.
Hints about death, repeated in the same chatAsks once whether you're thinking about death, and if so, shows crisis resources.
“I want to die,” “I want to disappear”Shows crisis resources right away, without asking first.
Talks about violence or being controlledPoints you to domestic violence and emergency services, and never suggests things like “try talking it out.”
“I keep waking up at 4 a.m. lately”Suggests a gentle place to start instead of an active step.

If you're in danger right now, please don't rely on Sowella AI. Call your local emergency number, or reach a crisis line: 988 (US & Canada), 116 123 (UK & Ireland), 13 11 14 (Australia), 1737 (New Zealand), 14416 (India), or find one near you at findahelpline.com. If you're facing violence or control at home, contact a local domestic violence helpline or emergency services.

Where fortune-telling stops and therapy can't reach

We built Sowella AI for the gap that fortune-telling leaves, and for the worries that never reach therapy because the first step feels too big.

Where saju and tarot fall short

  • No clear next step — They may tell you about timing, luck or direction, but often not what to actually do.
  • Vague, and open to interpretation — The same result can mean different things depending on who reads it, so it's hard to know if it fits you.
  • No check-in on your story — Readings come from your birth date or the cards, with no step that asks why things feel hard for you.
  • Hard to follow up — A reading usually ends there, so there's no way to see how it went and adjust.

Worries that therapy doesn't always reach

Psychotherapy is clinical care for diagnosed symptoms, so worries like these can be hard to bring there, or fall through the gaps.

  • Worries that don't feel “clinical” — “Should I change jobs?” or “Does my manager dislike me?” aren't symptoms, so a clinic can feel like too big a step.
  • A light place to start — Booking, per-session costs and waitlists make it hard to just say the hard thing out loud first.
  • Practice between sessions — With weekly sessions, no one helps you keep up in daily life in between.
  • Keeping it up afterwards — Once sessions end, there's rarely an easy way to check in on how you're doing.

How Sowella AI helps

  • It checks the reason, then gets specific — It confirms the reason behind your worry using proven psychology, then suggests steps that fit you now, starting light.
  • Start any time, lightly — 3 days free when you sign up, then US$9.99 for 3 months (about US$3.33 a month), so you can say the hard thing out loud first.
  • It stays with you between sessions and after — Between 1:1 wellness sessions, it checks in on your steps using the same approach, and keeps things light after you graduate.

Frequently asked questions

What is Sowella AI?

Sowella AI is the AI wellness coaching chat made by SSOL Wellness House: put simply, a wellness coach in your pocket. It helps you sort through worries that are on your mind but don't feel like a reason to see a doctor, and turns them into a small step you can take today. It's non-clinical wellness coaching, not diagnosis or treatment. A database designed and reviewed by counseling psychology experts decides which approach to use, what to ask and which steps to suggest.

What can I talk to Sowella AI about?

Everyday worries: career (should I leave or stay?), love (why do I keep meeting the same kind of person?), relationships at work, yourself and your life direction. You can also bring up things that are hard to say out loud, like money, family or comparing yourself to others. It's not for symptoms that need a diagnosis; it helps you sort through what's weighing on you and find small steps.

Does Sowella AI replace a doctor or therapy?

No. Sowella AI doesn't diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it doesn't replace a doctor, psychotherapy or counseling. If there are signs that you may need medical or clinical care, such as a threat to your safety or very deep distress, it stops coaching and points you to professional services.

How is Sowella AI different from therapy at a clinic or center?

Psychotherapy is clinical care, where a professional assesses and treats diagnosed symptoms or mental health conditions. Sowella AI is non-clinical wellness coaching for everyday worries that come before that point: it confirms the reason behind your worry, then suggests small steps that fit you now, one at a time. If you suspect symptoms, or daily life feels like it's falling apart, please see a professional first.

How is Sowella AI different from saju or tarot?

Saju and tarot readings vary with interpretation and often stop at timing or direction, without saying what to actually do. Sowella AI uses proven psychology to pinpoint the reason behind your worry, then suggests specific steps that fit that reason, starting light (e.g., stretch → walk → run).

How is Sowella AI different from a general AI chatbot?

Sowella AI is guided by 14+ psychology frameworks and an expert-reviewed database, and it draws on your tests, reports, past chats and session notes. General chatbots usually aren't built around this kind of coaching design and safety process.
