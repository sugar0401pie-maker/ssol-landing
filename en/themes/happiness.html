Who it suits

  • You do and achieve a lot, but don't feel happy

  • Feel happiness more often and more strongly in daily life
  • Gain the strength to turn hardship into an asset
  • Feel more satisfied in your relationships

Results vary from person to person.

Why it matters

A happy everyday life is actually not as hard as it sounds. It takes knowing how, and some effort. But many people mistake trying not to be unhappy for the way to be happy. Others know how to become happier in their heads, yet find it hard to try because of old habits.

Happiness researcher Ed Diener showed that the channels for negative and positive emotions are different. Our instincts are tuned to avoid feelings like worry, but building happiness takes a different, conscious kind of effort.

What SSOL Wellness House does

  • Look at the context of your life and analyze why happiness is missing
  • Carefully find the points where you can feel happy
  • Raise your well-being with creative, fun training

Research note

After professional positive psychology programs, happiness-related scores rose from the average (50th percentile) to between the top 35% and the top 19%.

Source: Carr, A., Cullen, K., Keeney, C., Canning, C., Mooney, O., Chinseallaigh, E., & O'Dowd, A. (2021). Effectiveness of positive psychology interventions: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

This research describes programs of this kind in general. A wellness session is training, not therapy.
