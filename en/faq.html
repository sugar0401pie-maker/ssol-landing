Frequently asked questions

Sowella AI

What is Sowella AI?

Sowella AI is the AI wellness coaching chat made by SSOL Wellness House: put simply, a wellness coach in your pocket. It helps you sort through worries that are on your mind but don't feel like a reason to see a doctor, and turns them into a small step you can take today. It's non-clinical wellness coaching, not diagnosis or treatment. A database designed and reviewed by counseling psychology experts decides which approach to use, what to ask and which steps to suggest.

What can I talk to Sowella AI about?

Everyday worries: career (should I leave or stay?), love (why do I keep meeting the same kind of person?), relationships at work, yourself and your life direction. You can also bring up things that are hard to say out loud, like money, family or comparing yourself to others. It's not for symptoms that need a diagnosis; it helps you sort through what's weighing on you and find small steps.

Does Sowella AI replace a doctor or therapy?

No. Sowella AI doesn't diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it doesn't replace a doctor, psychotherapy or counseling. If there are signs that you may need medical or clinical care, such as a threat to your safety or very deep distress, it stops coaching and points you to professional services.

How is Sowella AI different from therapy at a clinic or center?

Psychotherapy is clinical care, where a professional assesses and treats diagnosed symptoms or mental health conditions. Sowella AI is non-clinical wellness coaching for everyday worries that come before that point: it confirms the reason behind your worry, then suggests small steps that fit you now, one at a time. If you suspect symptoms, or daily life feels like it's falling apart, please see a professional first.

How is Sowella AI different from saju or tarot?

Saju and tarot readings vary with interpretation and often stop at timing or direction, without saying what to actually do. Sowella AI uses proven psychology to pinpoint the reason behind your worry, then suggests specific steps that fit that reason, starting light (e.g., stretch → walk → run).

How is Sowella AI different from a general AI chatbot?

Sowella AI is guided by 14+ psychology frameworks and an expert-reviewed database, and it draws on your tests, reports, past chats and session notes. Safety rules come first in every chat, it suggests steps matched to the reason you confirmed, and it can connect you to wellness sessions with a trainer. General chatbots usually aren't built around this kind of coaching design and safety process.

Can I try Sowella AI for free?

Yes. Sign up on the web and you can use Sowella AI free for 3 days. A SSOL Astro House star report also comes with a 3-day Sowella AI pass.

At launch prices: 1 month US$3.99, 3 months US$9.99 (about US$3.33 a month) and 12 months US$24.99 (about US$2.08 a month). We keep the door low so you can start and keep going without pressure. Prices are in USD, and your bank may add a foreign-transaction fee.

How soon does Sowella AI get to the heart of my worry?

It aims to reach the heart of your worry within three chats. It follows a simple path, Open up → Clarify → Reframe → Commit, and ends with one step you can take today. If you want, you can continue with a wellness session with a real person.

How does a Sowella AI chat work, step by step?

It narrows the possible reasons behind your worry one or two at a time, checking with you: “It sounds like… does that fit?” It only works with what you confirm, then chooses the approach and small steps that fit that reason. Like a coach, it forms a hunch and checks it with you before suggesting anything.

Does Sowella AI remember past chats?

Yes. It remembers your test results, in-depth reports, what you first shared, past chats and session notes, so you never have to start from scratch. It treats a reason as likely only when several of these point the same way, and your own answers always come first.

Can I use Sowella AI in a crisis?

Sowella AI isn't an emergency or crisis service. If you're in danger right now, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line, such as 988 (US & Canada), 116 123 (UK & Ireland) or 13 11 14 (Australia), or find one at findahelpline.com. Sowella AI applies safety rules first in every chat, and when it sees warning signs, it shows crisis resources, either right away or after checking with you once.

Can Sowella AI help with worries that saju, tarot or therapy didn't solve?

Saju and tarot are often vague and don't spell out a next step, and therapy is clinical care for diagnosed symptoms, so everyday worries that don't feel clinical can be hard to start with. Sowella AI works in that gap: it confirms the reason, suggests specific steps one at a time, and keeps you on track between sessions and after you graduate. If you suspect symptoms, or daily life feels like it's falling apart, please see a professional first.

What is Sowella AI based on?

It draws on 14+ psychology frameworks, including ACT, CBT, REBT, schema and emotion-focused approaches, plus 24+ techniques, 1,166+ small-step suggestions and 884+ guided questions. Every piece of content it learns from is reviewed by counseling psychology experts, and steps that don't fit the reason are filtered out in advance.

Which psychology frameworks does Sowella AI use?

The main frameworks Sowella AI draws on are acceptance and commitment (ACT), compassion-focused (CFT), dialectical behavior skills (DBT skills), cognitive-behavioral (CBT), rational emotive behavior (REBT), rational behavior training (RBT), the schema approach, the lifetrap approach, the interpersonal approach (IPT), well-being (WBT), positive psychology (PPT), emotion-focused (EFT), logotherapy and meaning-centered approaches (MCP), and we keep adding more. Each area of concern has a main and supporting framework (CBT for career, EFT for love, schema for relationships, WBT for yourself, ACT for life direction), and once the reason is confirmed, it suggests steps that fit that framework. Sowella AI uses ideas and exercises from these frameworks for guided self-reflection; it does not provide therapy.

1:1 wellness sessions

A 20-min first consultation and check-in makes sure the sessions fit you. Then you set a main mission and meet weekly for 8 sessions. Between sessions, Sowella AI helps you keep up your steps using the same approach. You graduate with your own tools and a plan to stay on track, and you can add a follow-up session 4 weeks later if you want.

How much do wellness sessions cost?

The 20-min first consultation is US$8.99, and sessions after that are US$59.99 per 50-min session (US$479.92 for 8).

Where, and in what language, are sessions held?

By video (Google Meet) or in person in Seoul (Gongdeok, Sangsu), in English or Korean. Times are shown in Korea time (KST) and your local time.

How are wellness sessions different from Sowella AI?

Wellness sessions are training with a human trainer: over 8 sessions you set a main mission and work toward real change. Sowella AI is an AI wellness coaching chat you can talk to any time, and between sessions it helps you keep up your steps using the same approach. Neither is medical diagnosis or treatment.
