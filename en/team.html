Frequently asked questions

Who made Sowella AI and the wellness tests?

CEO Junseok Kim (Counseling Psychologist, Level 2 · Youth Counselor, Level 2) and CSO Hyejin Kwak (Industrial Psychology Counselor, Level 1) design and review the tests, sessions and content together. Expert Advisor Semin Oh (Mental Health Clinical Psychologist, Level 1) reviews the interpretation database and safety standards, and Expert Advisor Yuju Oh (Integrative Arts Psychology Instructor, Level 1) reviews small steps and chat lines. All certifications are Korean.

Who reviews the content?

Everything Sowella AI learns from is reviewed by counseling psychology experts. Junseok Kim and Hyejin Kwak review content together, Semin Oh reviews the interpretation database and safety standards, and Yuju Oh reviews small steps and chat lines.

Who runs the 1:1 wellness sessions?

Our mental wellness trainers. Trainers who studied clinical and counseling psychology and arts therapy each take sessions in their own area, alongside a wider pool of experts including our advisory board.
