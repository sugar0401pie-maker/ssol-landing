How it works

  1. 120-min first consultation and check-in · First, we make sure the sessions are a good fit for you
  2. 2Set your main mission · Together, we choose 1–2 goals for the 8 sessions and how you'll know you've reached them
  3. 3Weekly sessions + quests in between · Sowella AI helps you keep up your small steps between sessions. A mission gauge (0–10) tracks every session, with a check-in at sessions 4 and 8
  4. 4Graduate and keep it going · You graduate with your own tools and a plan to stay on track. If you want, add a follow-up session 4 weeks later

Every session follows SSOL's wellness manual

Our own manual sets a main and supporting approach for each area of concern and shapes all 8 sessions. Adjustments for each person are reviewed at our weekly team roundtable. Between sessions, Sowella AI follows up on your small steps using the same approach.

Session themes

  • Career · Excellence over perfection — Turn performance anxiety and self-criticism into energy for excellence
  • Love · Change your dating patterns — Notice the scenes that keep repeating, and say what you really feel in one sentence
  • Group · Group wellness sessions — 8–10 people talking about life and people through psychology
  • Self · Train for happiness — Grow the good moments, and be a little kinder to yourself
  • Life · Find your direction — Discover what gives your life meaning, starting with the smallest first step

What we check first

In your first consultation, we look at how you're doing together. If you're in crisis, we'll point you to emergency and crisis services first, instead of a wellness session.

If you're in danger right now, please don't rely on Sowella AI. Call your local emergency number, or reach a crisis line: 988 (US & Canada), 116 123 (UK & Ireland), 13 11 14 (Australia), 1737 (New Zealand), 14416 (India), or find one near you at findahelpline.com. Frequently asked questions

How do 1:1 wellness sessions work?

A 20-min first consultation and check-in makes sure the sessions fit you. Then you set a main mission and meet weekly for 8 sessions. Between sessions, Sowella AI helps you keep up your steps using the same approach. You graduate with your own tools and a plan to stay on track, and you can add a follow-up session 4 weeks later if you want.

How much do wellness sessions cost?

The 20-min first consultation is US$8.99, and sessions after that are US$59.99 per 50-min session (US$479.92 for 8).

Where, and in what language, are sessions held?

By video (Google Meet) or in person in Seoul (Gongdeok, Sangsu), in English or Korean. Times are shown in Korea time (KST) and your local time.

How are wellness sessions different from Sowella AI?

Wellness sessions are training with a human trainer: over 8 sessions you set a main mission and work toward real change. Sowella AI is an AI wellness coaching chat you can talk to any time, and between sessions it helps you keep up your steps using the same approach. Neither is medical diagnosis or treatment.

What if I'm in crisis? Can I still book a wellness session?

In your first consultation, we look at how you're doing together. If you're in crisis, we'll point you to emergency and crisis services first, instead of a wellness session. If you're in danger right now, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line for your country, or find one at findahelpline.com.
