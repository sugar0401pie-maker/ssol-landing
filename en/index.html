Safety first

Every message passes through a safety check first. Step 3 · Go deeper with an expert

1:1 wellness sessions
with a mental wellness trainer

A wellness session isn't therapy. It's training for your mind. Like working with a personal trainer, you set your own main mission over 8 sessions and experience real change. The goal: reach a fuller life with wisdom from science and philosophy.

To reach the same result, the top 25% of practitioners needed 2 sessions, while the bottom 25% needed 10 (Mahon, Minami & Brown, 2025). Who you work with changes how much a session helps. SSOL holds its sessions to the standard of that top 25%: we check our trainers through skills assessments and our sessions by measuring progress every time.

How it works

  1. 120-min first consultation and check-in · First, we make sure the sessions are a good fit for you
  2. 2Set your main mission · Together, we choose 1–2 goals for the 8 sessions and how you'll know you've reached them
  3. 3Weekly sessions + quests in between · Sowella AI helps you keep up your small steps between sessions. A mission gauge (0–10) tracks every session, with a check-in at sessions 4 and 8
  4. 4Graduate and keep it going · You graduate with your own tools and a plan to stay on track. If you want, add a follow-up session 4 weeks later
Every session follows SSOL's wellness manualOur own manual sets a main and supporting approach for each area of concern and shapes all 8 sessions. Adjustments for each person are reviewed at our weekly team roundtable. Between sessions, Sowella AI follows up on your small steps using the same approach.
